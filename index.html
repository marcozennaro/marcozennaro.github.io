About

About me

Marco Zennaro is a Research Scientist at the Abdus Salam International Centre for Theoretical Physics (ICTP) in Trieste, Italy, where he coordinates the Science, Technology and Innovation Unit. He is also a Visiting Professor at the Kobe Institute of Computing (KIC) in Kobe, Japan.

His research is in ICT4D, the use of Information and Communication Technologies for Development, and in particular the Internet of Things for Development (IoT4D): wireless sensor networks and their applications in developing countries, from environmental monitoring to energy management. Over the years he has organized more than 30 training activities on IoT, wireless networking and TinyML in developing countries, and he is the lead organizer of the SciTinyML workshop series with Harvard University.

He received his PhD from KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm, Sweden, and his MSc in Electronic Engineering from the University of Trieste, Italy.
